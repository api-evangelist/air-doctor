There’s something undeniably magical about summer in Europe. It’s the promise of long, sun-drenched days, lazy al fresco meals that stretch into the evening, and the vibrant hum of cities coming alive under a warm sky. It’s a classic for a reason, but let’s be honest: summer also brings heat, crowds, and sometimes higher prices.
